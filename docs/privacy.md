# Akatabo privacy policy

Akatabo is a money tracker. This policy says what it stores, where, and what it never does.

## What Akatabo stores, and where

- Your entries (amounts, dates, descriptions, categories, accounts, fees, tax, and any foreign currency and rate you type), your budgets, regular payments, and your settings are stored in a database on your phone.
- The rates you type for other currencies are stored on your phone too.
- Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Akatabo has no account, no sign-in, and no server.

## What Akatabo never does

- It does not collect analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
- It does not show adverts and does not track you.
- It does not ask for the internet permission in the release app, so it cannot send your data anywhere on its own.

## Backups and exports

- A backup is a file you save where you choose. If you set a password, the backup is encrypted with it. We cannot recover a lost password.
- A PDF or CSV export is a file you create and share yourself. Once you share it, it is out of Akatabo's hands.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Notifications**: to show your reminders. You can turn them off in the phone's settings.
- **Fingerprint or face**: to unlock the app, if you turn on the app lock. The fingerprint data stays with your phone; Akatabo only receives a yes or no.

The app shows which permissions are allowed, under Settings > Phone permissions.

## Children

Akatabo does not collect information from anyone, so it does not collect information from children either.
